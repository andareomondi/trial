History

AIC Macedonia began its journey as AIC Jamcity, a dedicated community focused on worship and fellowship. This early phase was marked by a deep sense of unity and dedication among its members, providing a spiritual refuge for many. The church's growth in both numbers and spiritual maturity laid a strong foundation for future expansion.

As the church transitioned to AIC Mavoko, it experienced a period of profound transformation and expansion. This phase emphasized outreach and community support, making AIC Mavoko a beacon of hope and service. The church's commitment to its members and the broader community earned it a reputation as a pillar of strength and compassion.

Today, AIC Macedonia stands as a testament to the enduring faith and resilience of its congregation. Evolving from a slam church to an estate church, it continues to uphold its mission of spreading love, faith, and hope. The community's unwavering dedication to spiritual growth and service solidifies AIC Macedonia's position as a cherished institution.
