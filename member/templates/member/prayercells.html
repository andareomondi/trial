What are prayercells?

These are sub sections of the prayer and interaction channel department of the church. Their aim is outreach to everyone despite their diffrent locations of stay.

How do they work?

They are all specifically and uniquely designed throughout the week across different residence. Members then meet to the agreed home, share a word, a meal if possible and a prayer. Time of meetup is usually from 5:30pm to 7:00pm depending on the hosting home.

Where to meet next?

During every Sunday service, an announcement is made concerning the prayer cells, in which the number of attended members is made clear and the next home to visit.

How to join?

Joining is quite easy, just identify the area of residence among the list of prayer cells areas and join the one that is near you. If the available ones aren't satisfactory to you, you can always join the general prayer cell held in the main church premises
